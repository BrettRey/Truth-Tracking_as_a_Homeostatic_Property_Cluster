- BBS 49: e219, https://doi.org/10.1017/S0140525X26104580. Open peer commentary on Futrell & Mahowald.
- Central note: `literature/nefdt-ladyman-2026-language-of-real-patterns.md`.

**Why this paper cares:** The commentary's closing move is exactly the truth-tracking question — LMs are "engineering devices created to optimise a next-token prediction task," and it is "open whether the patterns they so expertly survey are the real ones in the heads of language users." That is the participation/grounding question in other words. Their "multiple-models idealisation" (Weisberg 2013) gives a principled way to say LLMs and formal theory track different real patterns for different purposes — useful for framing *what* LLMs participate in without over- or under-claiming.

**Possible use:** situate the projectible-profile account against the real-patterns literature; cite N&L for the domain question (outputs vs cognition) and for the multiple-models framing.
