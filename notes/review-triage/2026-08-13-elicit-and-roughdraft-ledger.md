# Elicit and Roughdraft review ledger

Date: 2026-08-13

This ledger records the disposition of Brett's Roughdraft annotations and the Elicit review. The Elicit report was treated as a referee report rather than as an instruction list. The venue is *Synthese*, and the article's contribution is conceptual and methodological; Online Resource 1 supplies design specifications and fake-data checks but reports no empirical results.

## Elicit review

### Adopted

- **State the philosophical target.** The introduction now identifies the decomposition as an account of epistemic architecture for explaining and predicting performance and guiding intervention, not a semantic theory or scalar metric.
- **Clarify the structure of the five features.** Section 4 now says that each is minimally necessary for the live corrective path, while degrees above that minimum interact and need not yield a total order.
- **Separate ex ante criteria from favourable outcomes.** The target, perturbation, and improvement criterion are declared before conditional repair and false-intervention rates are assessed.
- **Operationalise checking-route independence.** The article and supplement now use conditional error overlap for the proposed test, report false interventions, and treat lineage and marginal correlation as evidence rather than definitions. The supplement adds held-out verification of the proposed matching variables.
- **Make auxiliary assumptions explicit.** The clinical prediction is conditional on task construction, route invocation and integration, and the data distribution.
- **Recast the experiments as validation arguments.** The article now says this explicitly. The supplement adds a rival-prediction matrix stating what each design tests and what the strongest coverage reply can absorb.
- **Sharpen the identification result.** The article now says that no behavioural contrast can defeat unrestricted effective coverage once it may encode every route relation and uptake policy. Effective coverage is reduced form; the decomposition is structural.
- **Stress-test the decomposition with constructed data.** A reproducible workflow now compares fixed input coverage, fixed conditional coverage, and the structural decomposition on held-out task--intervention combinations. It includes worlds in which the decomposition wins, conditional coverage is sufficient, feature measurement defeats the true latent decomposition, and predictive surplus fails to improve intervention choice.
- **Make joint structure explicit.** A bounded Alloy audit found that separately instantiated features need not form a connected correction path. Section 4 now requires joint instantiation along such a path; the model and reproducible checks remain internal analysis artifacts.

### Already answered

- **Empirical-design confounds.** Online Resource 1 already specifies paired item construction, a crossed design, baseline accuracy as a covariate rather than proof of matching, blind scoring, the estimand, thresholds, uncertainty, route invocation checks, and failure conditions. The new matching-verification paragraph closes the remaining checker-design gap.
- **Capacity versus performance.** Section 4 already distinguishes capacity, exercise frequency, conditional repair, and false intervention.
- **Truth-tracking versus Nozick.** Section 2 explicitly limits the paper's use to patterned support for representational success and distinguishes Nozick's subjunctive account of knowledge.
- **Coverage overlap.** Section 5.7 already distinguishes input, conditional, and effective coverage and concedes the identification limit.

### Declined as stated

- **A simulation that produces a uniquely discriminating behavioural result against unrestricted coverage.** For any finite mapping from architecture and environment to performance, an unrestricted coverage variable can be defined to reproduce it. The implemented fake-data exercise therefore has an asymmetric role: it compares prespecified restricted models and exposes non-trivial failures, but doesn't claim to defeat unrestricted effective coverage.
- **A new compact table of answerability, truth-tracking, reliability, and corrective control in the article.** The manuscript has already undergone terminological triage, and those relations are stated in prose where they are introduced and compared. Another global crosswalk would restore the cognitive load recently removed.
- **Experimental results as a condition of publication.** This is a conceptual and methodological submission. The supplement makes the programme operational and falsifiable without representing proposed studies as completed validation.
- **The alleged PDF defects `ro-bust`, `envir-onment`, and `tar-get`.** These are text-extraction renderings of ordinary line-end hyphenation, not malformed words in the source or visible PDF. The PDF will still receive a final visual and extraction check after this batch.
- **Fedorenko on the language network or language and thought in the grounding-literature paragraph.** That work bears on the dissociation of linguistic competence from non-linguistic cognition, not on the grounding taxonomy being summarized there. Mahowald et al. already appears where that distinction does argumentative work.

### Answered without further change

- **Profile versus arrangement.** An arrangement is the target- and task-indexed output-producing system; its profile is the description of which routes constrain it and how they are related. The revised coverage wording preserves this distinction.
