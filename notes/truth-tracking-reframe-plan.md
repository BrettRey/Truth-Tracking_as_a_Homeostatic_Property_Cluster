# Truth-Tracking Reframe Plan

## Proposed Change

Reframe the truth-tracking paper away from the strong headline claim that truth-tracking is itself a homeostatic property cluster. The revised frame should treat truth-tracking successes as stabilised by a projectible profile of partially coupled mechanisms. Large language models make the profile visible because they participate strongly in some stabilizers, especially testimony and coherence, while lacking or weakening others, especially perception, verification, and world-directed corrective feedback.

## What Stays

- Keep Boyd and HPC as the lineage and pressure point.
- Keep the LLM hallucination payoff as the main contribution.
- Keep Khalidi-style network vocabulary where it helps describe mechanism connectivity.
- Keep the non-relabeling test: the paper must say what the profile view predicts or explains that reliabilism, coherentism, pragmatism, and correspondence theories do not.

## What Changes

- Retitle the paper from the old HPC-centered title to a profile-centered title.
- Replace strong claims that truth-tracking is an HPC with weaker claims that truth-tracking successes are stabilised by a projectible profile whose stabilizers can be more or less coupled.
- Reserve `homeostatic` for cases where corrective coupling has been shown, following the discipline of `Not every stable cluster is homeostatic`.
- Recast Section 3 as `Truth-tracking as a projectible mechanism profile`.
- Recast Section 4 as a projectibility-first section rather than a homeostasis-first section.
- Recast Section 5 around what profile structure does that component theories do not.
- Cite the other paper where the revised draft explicitly distinguishes stable/projectible profiles from homeostatic ones.

## Candidate Title

`Truth-Tracking Profiles: What LLMs Participate In`

Alternatives:

- `Truth-Tracking as a Projectible Profile: What LLMs Participate In`
- `Partial Truth-Tracking: Stabilizing Mechanisms in Humans and LLMs`
- `What LLMs Participate In: Truth-Tracking Profiles and Partial Grounding`

## Expected Manuscript Effect

The revised paper should no longer need to prove that truth-tracking is literally homeostatic before the LLM argument works. Its core claim becomes more defensible: truth-tracking is projectible because multiple stabilizers tend to co-vary under ordinary human and institutional conditions; LLMs reveal the structure by separating those stabilizers.

## Citation To Add If Needed

Use the other paper as an internal portfolio anchor only where useful:

```latex
\citep{reynolds2026notEveryStableCluster}
```

The citation should support the methodological point that stable or projectible profiles should not automatically be called homeostatic. It should not become a crutch for the main LLM argument.
