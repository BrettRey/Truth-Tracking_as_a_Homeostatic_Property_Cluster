# Boyd 2019 Source Note

Source: Richard Boyd, "Rethinking natural kinds, reference and truth: towards more correspondence with reality, not less," Synthese 198(Suppl 12), S2863-S2903. DOI: 10.1007/s11229-019-02138-4. Issue citation is 2021; the PDF states received 20 April 2018, accepted 11 February 2019, and published online 23 February 2019.

Local files: `literature/boyd_2019_rethinking_natural_kinds_reference_truth.pdf` and `literature/boyd_2019_rethinking_natural_kinds_reference_truth.md`.

## Core Takeaway

Boyd is not just background for HPC natural kinds. This paper already generalizes the accommodationist picture to reference and truth. The abstract states the high-level thesis: natural-kind semantics is a special case of naturalistic signaling, and reference and truth are special cases of accommodation between signaling practices and causally relevant world-structures (local MD lines 11-15).

That means this paper cannot win by merely saying that truth-tracking is a cluster phenomenon. Boyd has already made the broad move: reference, truth, and natural kinds belong inside a naturalistic account of how representational practices come to be epistemically reliable.

## What Boyd Gives Us

First, Boyd makes metaphysics, epistemology, and semantics continuous with empirical causal explanation. The task is to explain how representational systems accurately track the world, not to analyze concepts a priori (lines 90-103).

Second, accommodation is the primary notion. Successful reference is an alignment between perceptual, instrumental, cognitive, and representational practices and relevant causal structures (lines 107-146). Reference is not fixed one term at a time; it emerges from whole disciplinary practices and their causal relations to the world (lines 132-146).

Third, definitions do not fix reference. Boyd treats natural-kind definitions as worldly property families or processes that help explain epistemic success, not as sentences or concepts that stipulate reference (lines 182-210). This matters because LLMs often inherit definitional surfaces without inheriting the surrounding epistemic machinery.

Fourth, reference itself is processual and often partial. Boyd explicitly rejects a static relation between terms and determinate kinds as the general model (lines 212-228). This is useful for LLMs, because the question should not be binary "refers/doesn't refer"; it should be how a system participates in accommodation processes.

Fifth, Boyd broadens truth beyond neat Tarski-style cases. Scientific practice can be epistemically productive even when some terms lack determinate reference or truth conditions (lines 250-282). He then states the crucial thesis: reference, partial denotation, and truth are special cases of a broader, temporally extended accommodation process (lines 308-316).

## Non-Relabeling Constraint

The novelty target should be narrow and diagnostic:

This paper should not present the truth-tracking profile as a replacement theory of truth, nor as a discovery that truth and reference are accommodated by causal structures. Boyd already owns that frame.

The paper's distinctive contribution should be the LLM case: a text-trained system can participate in some truth-stabilizing mechanisms while missing or weakening others. It can inherit linguistic accommodation, testimonial patterns, inferential associations, calibration signals, and user feedback without direct perceptual, instrumental, or action-based coupling. The profile claim earns its keep by explaining uneven participation and its failure signature, not by renaming truth.

The relabeling test for Section 1:

If "truth-tracking has a projectible profile" only means "truth depends on many mechanisms," the paper fails. If it predicts and explains why LLMs can be semantically fluent, often accurate, and still systematically vulnerable when coherence/testimony mechanisms outrun perception, instrumentation, intervention, and correction mechanisms, the paper has an independent role.

## Drafting Implications

Section 1 should explicitly acknowledge Boyd's priority. A usable opening contrast:

Boyd gives the accommodationist background: reference and truth are stabilizing achievements of representational practices embedded in causal relations to the world. The present paper asks what happens when an artificial text system enters some of those practices without entering all of them.

The core claim should be mechanism-profile language:

Truth-tracking is not a single capacity that an LLM either has or lacks. The relevant successes are stabilised by a projectible profile of mechanisms: perception, measurement, action, testimony, coherence, calibration, correction, and institutional feedback. LLMs expose the profile structure because they dissociate several of these stabilizers.

This also gives the paper a prediction:

Failures should concentrate where linguistic coherence and social uptake provide strong local signals while independent world-coupling and error-correction signals are weak, stale, or absent. Retrieval, tool use, embodiment, expert review, and iterative feedback should matter because they add specific missing stabilizers, not because they magically "ground" the model as a whole.

## Cautions

Boyd is not writing about LLMs. Any claim about LLMs is an extension from his accommodationist framework.

Do not collapse Boyd's "accommodation" into generic usefulness. He is defending correspondence with reality through causal alignment in practice, including messy cases where reference and truth are partial or not yet determinate.

Do not treat operational definitions as epistemically shallow. Boyd's reply to Ereshefsky and Reydon is that operational definitions can be exactly what a practice should use when they provide access to the relevant HPC kinds (lines 525-531).
