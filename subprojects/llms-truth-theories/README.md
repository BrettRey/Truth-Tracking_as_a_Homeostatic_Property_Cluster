# LLMs and Theories of Truth

Working title: **What LLMs Tell Us About Theories of Truth**

This subproject incubates the inverse paper. The main manuscript uses theories of
truth-tracking to diagnose LLMs. This paper uses LLMs as dissociation cases for theories
of truth and truth-tracking.

## Core Thesis

LLMs reveal that familiar theories of truth and truth-tracking are often best read as
isolating stabilisers that ordinarily travel together in human epistemic life.
Correspondence foregrounds answerability, coherentism foregrounds inferential and
discursive integration, pragmatism foregrounds practical correction, reliabilism
foregrounds stable production, and social epistemology foregrounds testimony and
institutional transmission. LLMs matter because they separate those stabilisers.

## Boundary From Paper 1

Paper 1 asks: **what truth-tracking profile do LLMs have?**

Paper 2 asks: **what do LLMs reveal about the division of labour among theories of

Paper 1 should keep the grounding/profile diagnostic as its centre. Paper 2 can take on
the truth-pluralism and alethic-functionalist literature directly.
