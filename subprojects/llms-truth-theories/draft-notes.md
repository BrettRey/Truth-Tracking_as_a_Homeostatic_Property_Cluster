# Draft Notes

## One-Sentence Pitch

LLMs reveal the division of labour among theories of truth by separating stabilisers
that human epistemic life normally bundles: answerability, coherence, practical
correction, reliable production, testimony, and social accountability.

## The Key Move

Don't lead with "theories of truth are partial." That framing is too familiar. Lead with
LLMs as dissociation instruments. They create a case where formal linguistic competence,
textual inheritance, and local coherence are strong while perception, measurement,
intervention, practical failure, and accountability are weak or derivative.

## What Paper 2 Adds

Alethic pluralists and functionalists already have the general thought that truth has
multiple roles or manifestations. The LLM contribution is a stress test: it shows how
the roles separate in a deployed representational system, and it lets us ask what
failures follow when some roles are present without others.

## Keep From Paper 1

- The distinction between truth and truth-relevant representational success.
- The answerability constraint.
- Participation as inherited, operational, corrective, or decorative.
- The architecture-sensitive comparison among text-only, retrieval, tools, multimodal,
  expert-reviewed, and action-guided systems.

## Keep Out of Paper 1

- Full engagement with alethic pluralism and functionalism.
- A survey of correspondence, coherence, pragmatism, reliabilism, and social
  epistemology as truth theories.
- Any claim that LLMs reveal the nature of truth itself.
