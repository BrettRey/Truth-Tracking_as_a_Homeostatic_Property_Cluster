# Working Outline

## Provisional Title

**What LLMs Tell Us About Theories of Truth**

Alternatives:

- **LLMs as Dissociation Cases for Truth-Tracking**
- **Truth Theories as Stabilisers: The LLM Dissociation**
- **Thin Truth, Thick Truth-Tracking**

## Abstract Shape

Large language models produce a distinctive philosophical pressure case. They can show
formal linguistic competence, inherit dense textual residues of testimony, and produce
locally coherent discourse while lacking or weakening perception, measurement,
intervention, practical correction, and ordinary social accountability. This paper
argues that the case reveals a division of labour among familiar theories of truth and
truth-tracking. Correspondence, coherentism, pragmatism, reliabilism, social
epistemology, and deflationism each isolate a real stabilising role. LLMs matter because
they separate those roles, showing that truth-tracking is better understood as a coupled
ecology than as the achievement of one stabiliser alone.

## Section Plan

### 1. The Dissociation Case

Start from the LLM puzzle: fluent, coherent, testimony-saturated outputs can fail to
remain answerable to the world. Treat the LLM as a dissociation instrument, not just as
an application case.

### 2. Why This Matters for Truth Theory

The target is truth-relevant representational success: what makes truth-ascription,
accuracy-ascription, and truth-tracking projectible across cases. The paper shouldn't
offer a new reductive theory of truth.

### 3. The Division of Labour

Map the stabilisers:

- Correspondence: answerability to how things are.
- Coherentism: inferential and discursive integration.
- Pragmatism: correction through action and success.
- Reliabilism: stable production under appropriate process individuation.
- Social epistemology: testimony, records, trust, roles, and institutions.
- Deflationism: the thin logical role of the truth predicate.

### 4. What LLMs Pull Apart

Show how text-only and weakly tooled LLMs can inherit textual residues and coherence
while weakening perception, measurement, intervention, and practical correction. Then
contrast retrieval, tools, multimodality, expert review, and action-guided systems.

### 5. Relation to Pluralism and Functionalism

Engage the likely referee objection: alethic pluralism and functionalism already claim
that truth has multiple roles or manifestations. The reply has to be precise: LLMs add a
dissociation case and a diagnostic/prospective method, not the bare claim of plurality.

### 6. Projection, Not Taxonomy

Avoid a static taxonomy of theories. The profile should support predictions about which
systems and domains will preserve, lose, or compensate for answerability when stabilisers
are separated.

### 7. Conclusion

LLMs don't ask us to choose among theories of truth. They ask why the phenomena those
theories emphasise normally travel together, and what happens when a system participates
in some of them without the others.
