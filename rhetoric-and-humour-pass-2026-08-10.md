# Rhetoric and humour pass: *Grounding Without Corrective Control*

## Verdict

The paper doesn't need more ornament. It needs a small number of figures that make its argumentative architecture easier to feel: the separation of reference from corrective control, the return path from discrepancy to changed production, and the difference between a route being named and a route being live.

The strongest treatment is therefore architectural rather than decorative:

1. bracket the paper with a matched comparison between the octopus and Izzy;
2. reuse one empirical case as the routes are added and removed;
3. promote the existing elimination of rival bearers into an early hinge;
4. replace some roadmap prose with transitions that state what has been earned and what remains to be shown; and
5. let three existing epigrammatic sentences carry more structural weight.

The humour should be dry, sparse, and self-applicative. The paper can afford two or three new moments. They should expose a category mistake or a nominal-but-inoperative route, never make an opponent or an LLM the butt of the joke.

## What is already saturated

The dominant figure is contrastive qualification: *rather than* appears 51 times, *isn't* 30 times, and *doesn't* 27 times. The paper also repeatedly uses “the question”, “come apart”, and “different”. Much of this is analytically necessary, but it means more antithesis, anaphora, tricolons, rhetorical questions, or “not X but Y” sentences would flatten the hierarchy of claims. Those common figures should be treated as spent resources.

The paper already has several excellent, less generic lines:

- “Errors enter by that route, and the account supplies none by which they leave” (lines 768–770).
- “A model trained that way has been corrected; it isn't thereby correctible” (line 1147).
- “the language of measurement without measurement, the language of citation without a record” (lines 1176–1177).
- Othello-GPT is “striking for that reason and unrepresentative for the same one” (lines 1306–1308).

These should become load-bearing returns, not receive nearby competitors.

## Recommended structural figures

### 1. Syncrisis with epanodos: make the octopus and Izzy bracket the argument

**Priority: high.** Syncrisis is a parallel comparison; epanodos returns to an earlier term or theme with added force. The paper opens with Bender and Koller's octopus (lines 80–86) and reaches Mandelkern and Linzen's Izzy only at lines 1452–1459. At present they are separate citations. Together they stage the exact distinction the paper needs: the two thought experiments pull in opposite directions on reference while sharing a sharply restricted checking architecture.

At the end of “What grounding leaves open”, add something like:

> The octopus and Izzy are used to pull in opposite directions on reference. Their shared limit is architectural. In each case, linguistic traffic is the principal route, so a discrepancy that never enters that traffic cannot alter present production. What isn't on the wire cannot correct what is.

Then return to the pair in one sentence in the conclusion. This gives the paper ring structure without a theatrical opening or a new thought experiment. The final sentence above is memorable, but it should appear only once.

### 2. Exergasia: make the lead case a running case rather than adding examples

**Priority: high.** Exergasia develops the same matter through successive treatments. The manuscript currently moves from the lead assay (lines 215–218), to a thermometer (lines 541–553), to an old weather webpage (lines 563–567), to a clinical retrieval design (lines 1516–1525). The variety proves generality, but it also makes the reader rebuild the arrangement each time.

Keep the lead/clinical case and return to it as routes are removed or restored. For example, the weather paragraph could become:

> Return to the lead assay. A value copied from yesterday's log may remain right while conditions stay fixed. A value produced through a live assay, calibration standard, and drift monitor survives a different range of perturbations because changes in the sample or instrument have routes into the result.

The same case can then mature into the crossed clinical design. The repeated object lets the reader watch the profile change rather than compare unrelated objects. Keep the ignored calibration monitor because it isolates capacity from realized effectiveness unusually well; the point is to retire the weather example, not the thermometer contrast.

### 3. Expeditio: promote the bearer elimination into an early hinge

**Priority: high.** Expeditio enumerates the candidate explanations and eliminates all but the one that survives. Lines 1032–1045 already do this very well: not the model, not the output, not the unrestricted surrounding practice; the bearer is the arrangement. But that resolution arrives after the paper has used *system*, *output*, and *arrangement* for many pages.

Move a compact version to the first profile declaration around lines 139–144:

> The bearer isn't the model, whose routes vary by task; the output, which has no routes; or the surrounding practice without limit, which would confer the credit of human science on every deployment. It is the target- and task-indexed arrangement that produces the output.

The later subsection can then deepen the result instead of announcing it. This is both a rhetorical gain and the cleanest repair to the remaining projectibility-bearer delay.

### 4. Metabasis: make section transitions state the proof state

**Priority: high.** Metabasis tells the reader what has been established and what the next part must establish. The existing roadmap at lines 180–191 is complete but mechanical, ending with “Section 8 concludes.” In a long conceptual paper, three argumentative hinges would orient better than a catalogue of sections.

Useful transition forms are:

- after the target section: “The target is now fixed. The next question is which features of its background make success travel.”
- before the component-theory section: “The profile now licenses a contrast. It earns its keep only if that contrast does work the component theories leave optional.”
- before the LLM section: “The objections have fixed the scope. The pressure case must now show whether that discipline predicts anything.”

These are not throat-clearing. Each names an unpaid argumentative debt. If adopted, shorten the roadmap to two sentences or remove it.

### 5. Epanorthosis: let grammatical self-correction enact conceptual correction

**Priority: medium-high.** Epanorthosis amends a formulation immediately after stating it. The corrected/correctible sentence at line 1147 is the paper's natural centre, but its current subject slightly blurs the bearer.

Sharpen it to:

> A model trained that way has been corrected—or, more exactly, its training process has. The deployed arrangement isn't thereby correctible.

The sentence corrects itself while distinguishing historical correction from a live corrective route. Use this figure once. Repeating the corrected/correctible morphology would turn a conceptual hinge into a slogan.

### 6. Epiphonema: promote three existing summaries

**Priority: medium.** An epiphonema is a compact sentence that gathers what precedes it. Three are already present. Give each its own short paragraph and, where possible, let it close a subsection:

- “Errors enter by that route, and the account supplies none by which they leave.”
- the revised corrected/correctible sentence;
- “The language of measurement without measurement; the language of citation without a record.”

They form a progression: inherited error, historical correction without live correction, and merely decorative participation. That progression is stronger than adding a new refrain.

### 7. Scesis onomaton as an optional local compression

**Priority: reserve only.** Scesis onomaton uses a sequence of verbless phrases. The retrieval-failure list at lines 1187–1194 is a good candidate because its current syntax makes three concrete failures feel like another taxonomy:

> A stale index. Half a record. A wording match without the answer. Each leaves the archival route nominally present and materially absent.

This gives one burst of pace after sustained conceptual prose. Don't repeat the device elsewhere.

## Humour pass

### The existing comic register

The paper already has dry conceptual wit. It works by taking architectural labels literally and asking whether the labelled route does anything. The ignored monitor, the checker-trained model that cannot check today's figure, the “language of measurement without measurement”, and the vendor evidence assessed for independence all belong to this register. Keep it. There is no need for jokes about hallucination, octopuses, bullshit, or LLM “confidence”; those are the first jokes any reader expects.

### Recommended additions

#### 1. Self-applicative urbanity in the vendor footnote

**Best addition.** At lines 1500–1504, the paper notes that both performance figures are a vendor's own report in a paper concerned with independent checking. Make the reflexive point explicit:

> Both figures are the vendor's own report about its own product. In a paper about independent checking, the evidence arrives with its own profile.

This is a civil, self-implicating joke: it applies the paper's standard to its own evidence and strengthens rather than interrupts the caveat.

#### 2. A paraprosdokian in the Frankfurt passage

**Good addition.** After contrasting indifference to truth with the absence of a route at lines 1355–1361:

> The profile diagnosis asks for a route, not a better attitude.

The unexpected final word produces a small laugh while clarifying why the architectural diagnosis yields intervention-specific predictions. It also avoids deciding whether an LLM literally has attitudes.

#### 3. Controlled bathos in the Othello passage

**Optional.** Before the qualification at lines 1306–1312:

> Othello's world fits in the stream because its world is a board.

The scale-drop is mildly comic, but the sentence also states exactly why the result doesn't transfer to open domains. Use this only if the surrounding paragraph is shortened; otherwise it restates the next two sentences.

#### 4. Deadpan literalism for an idle checker

**Optional alternative to the Othello line.** Around lines 1163–1168:

> A checker that is available but never consulted belongs to the product description, not the correction loop.

This gives “decorative participation” a concrete administrative flavour. Because it uses another *not X* contrast, adopt it only if a nearby contrastive sentence is cut.

### Humour to preserve rather than amplify

- “Errors enter … none … leave” is already funny in the right dry register. Don't add a punchline.
- “Corrected / correctible” has wit because morphology carries the argument. Don't repeat it.
- The Ruyant “prop” passage is strongest when scrupulously fair. “Once the prop becomes a dissertation…” is tempting, but it risks sounding as though the paper is laughing at the view rather than extending it.
- The approval-rate discussion is inherently absurd enough. Let the numbers carry it.

### Humour guardrails

- No humour in the abstract, the five-feature declaration, the ten-point threshold, or the defeat conditions.
- Aim humour upward or inward: at product labels, evidential arrangements, or the paper's own predicament. Don't aim it at named interlocutors.
- Avoid anthropomorphic jokes about what a model “wants”, “believes”, or “cares about”; the Frankfurt section makes those attributions philosophically live.
- Avoid puns on *truth*, *grounding*, and *tracking*. They are cheap precisely where the paper needs terminological control.
- Add at most two of the four proposed moments. The vendor line plus the Frankfurt line is the best pair.

## Projectibility guardrail

The rhetoric should make the projection easier to identify, not upgrade the worldly claim. The current audit is:

| Check | Status | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Declaration | YELLOW | The full retrieval declaration appears only at lines 1545–1570; the abstract and introduction give the interaction but not its full bearer, population, tolerance, and failure conditions. |
| Non-trivial projection | GREEN | Route-by-task transfer and the live-update contrast go beyond the facts used to classify the routes (lines 1233–1239 and 1616–1627). |
| Warrant vs world-side claim | YELLOW | The manuscript explicitly separates warrant, stability, dependence, maintenance, and control, but “Why the profile is projectible” still places the warrant predicate on the worldly profile rather than on a bounded claim. |
| World-side order | GREEN | Stability and directed dependence are incomparable; maintenance and corrective control are separately stronger (lines 468–489). |
| Stabilizer vs controller | YELLOW | Most of the paper reserves corrective control appropriately. The conclusion's “actively maintained … homeostatic” formulation at lines 1662–1664 risks making maintenance sufficient for homeostasis. |
| Level and mereology | GREEN, late | The arrangement is the right bearer and is carefully fixed at lines 1032–1045, but that clarification should come earlier. |
| Scope and field-relativity | GREEN | Purpose selects the question; declared projections remain world-answerable and refutable (lines 853–891). |
| Prospective demotion | GREEN | The task classes are frozen, the threshold is declared, and revision after seeing the effect is counted as a result (lines 1545–1570). |
| Positioning and conclusion | GREEN | The payoff is route-specific transfer, not kindhood or mechanism description alone (lines 1682–1691). |

One rhetorical sentence should therefore **not** be added: anything like “a stable profile becomes homeostatic when its links are strong enough.” Strength, density, and maintenance don't establish corrective control. If the conclusion keeps *homeostatic*, it should name perturbation-sensitive detection and repair, not merely active maintenance.

## Minimal adoption package

If only one pass is made, I would do exactly this:

1. add the octopus–Izzy syncrisis and return to it once in the conclusion;
2. move the bearer expeditio into the introduction;
3. add the three metabasis transitions and shorten the mechanical roadmap;
4. turn the three existing epiphonemata into visible subsection closes;
5. add the vendor-evidence line and the Frankfurt “route, not attitude” line;
6. change the conclusion's *homeostatic* sentence so corrective control, not maintenance alone, licenses the term.

That is enough. Adding all the optional figures would make the paper sound worked-over rather than controlled.
